# Eliza Duties and Responsibilities

- **Character Ingestion**: Load, parse, and instantiate character profiles defining personality, knowledge, lore, and communication styles.
- **Multi-Platform Dispatch**: Ingest messages from Discord, Twitter, Telegram, Direct API, and client frontends.
- **Memory & RAG Retrieval**: Index messages, compute embedding vectors, and retrieve relevant conversational memories.
- **Action Evaluation**: Match incoming user requests against available character actions, plugins, and evaluators.
- **On-Chain Execution**: Coordinate safe blockchain interactions including balance checks, swaps, and NFT minting via trusted plugins.
